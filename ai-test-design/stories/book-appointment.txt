Title: Book a doctor appointment

As a signed-in patient, I want to book a time slot with a doctor and give an optional reason, so that I have a confirmed appointment I can see later.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Only a signed-in user can book an appointment; a request without a valid login is rejected.
- A time slot must be selected; booking without one is rejected with the message "Select a time slot to continue."
- The chosen slot must be one of the slots offered by the chosen doctor; any other slot is rejected with "That slot isn't available for this doctor."
- The reason is optional; an appointment can be booked with or without it.
- A successfully booked appointment appears in the user's own appointment list, and not in any other user's list.
