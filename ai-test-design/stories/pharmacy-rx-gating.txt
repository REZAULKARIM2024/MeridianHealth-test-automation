Title: Order medicine with prescription gating

As a signed-in patient, I want to order medicines from the pharmacy, so that I receive them at my address, while prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Only a signed-in user can place an order.
- An order containing at least one prescription-only (Rx) item is rejected with "Upload a valid prescription for Rx items in your cart." unless the prescription has been confirmed.
- An order containing only non-Rx items can be placed without a prescription.
- A placed order appears in the user's own order list, and not in any other user's list.
